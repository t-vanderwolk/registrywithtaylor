## Bonus: pitch a new section
**The Trend I'm Spotting, a weekly editor's take.** Once a week I call out one shift I'm seeing across registries and new parenthood, from "investing in support" to whatever's rising next, in a short, opinionated blurb. Here's why it works. Readers trust a knowledgeable friend who actually has a point of view, and a named, recurring POV builds a habit and a real reason to open.
